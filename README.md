**A centralized digital platform for campus complaints, student feedback, and institutional improvement.**

The **Campus Complaint & Feedback Portal** is a college project designed to simplify how students communicate campus-related issues, share feedback, and report concerns. It aims to provide a structured digital platform that improves communication between students and college administration.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Provide a centralized platform for campus-related complaints and feedback.
- Simplify the process of reporting student concerns.
- Improve communication between students and college administration.
- Support transparent complaint tracking and management.
- Encourage constructive feedback for continuous campus improvement.

## ✨ Planned Features

### 📝 Complaint Management
Allow students to submit campus-related complaints and organize reported issues in a structured manner.

### 💬 Student Feedback
Provide a platform where students can share suggestions, opinions, and ideas to improve campus facilities and services.

### 📊 Complaint Status Tracking
Enable students to monitor the progress of their complaints and check their resolution status.

### 🛡️ Administrative Dashboard
Provide administrators with a centralized interface to review complaints, manage feedback, and update complaint statuses.

### 📱 Responsive Interface
Design a user-friendly interface that works across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.

### 🔐 User Access Management
Organize platform access according to user roles, such as students and administrators.

*Note: These features represent the planned project scope and may change during development.*
